# Customer Satisfaction Analysis

## Project Overview
Customer Satisfaction Analysis is a Data Analytics project focused on analyzing customer feedback and survey responses to understand satisfaction levels, identify common customer concerns, and evaluate customer experience.

The project uses Python to explore customer ratings, satisfaction patterns, issue categories, product categories, purchase channels, regions, and issue-resolution performance.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze overall customer satisfaction levels.
- Understand customer rating distribution.
- Identify frequently reported customer issues.
- Evaluate satisfaction across product categories and regions.
- Analyze purchase channel performance.
- Examine issue resolution and response times.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Dataset Information
- Total Records: 2,500
- Total Columns: 15
- Survey Period: January–December 2025
- Missing Values: 0
- Duplicate Response IDs: 0

**Note:** This is a synthetic dataset generated for educational and portfolio purposes. It does not contain real customer survey responses.

## Business Recommendations
- Prioritize frequently reported customer issues.
- Investigate low-rated product categories.
- Improve customer support response and resolution processes.
- Monitor satisfaction across regions and purchase channels.
- Track customer satisfaction trends regularly.
- Review recurring negative feedback to identify improvement opportunities.
